HOW TO PLAY & LOAD SONGS

⚡ 1. YOU PLAY THE MELODY (GUITAR HERO STYLE)

• Glowing triangle notes fly out of the 3D ANUBAYN Δ portal down to the 7 A-N-U-B-A-Y-N letters.
• Tap or slide your thumb onto the glowing letter right as the note reaches the ring! When you hit it, YOU play the lead guitar/synth melody of the song. If you miss it, the melody drops out!

📂 2. PLAY FULL SONGS FROM YOUR IPHONE

Why Spotify cut off after 30s: Spotify & Apple Music lock their web players to 30-second previews outside their apps and block games from reading the beat.
To play a full song from start to finish: Load any MP3 / M4A / WAV song saved in your iPhone Files app! The game reads the song's real bass & melody beats, spawns notes down the highway, and muffles the song if you miss notes—just like Guitar Hero!

🎙️ 3. SING OR SYNC TO A SPEAKER

• Sing into Mic: Singing or humming shows your vocal note inside the 3D Δ logo and activates Vocal Star Power (auto-catches notes for 2x points!).
• Playing Spotify/Apple Music on another speaker/TV/computer? Tap below so your iPhone mic listens to the room speaker and spawns notes to the live beat!
